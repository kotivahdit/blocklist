# Removal requests

If your address is on one of our lists and you believe it should not be:

1. **Wait – or fix and wait.** Every IP is removed automatically after **7 days without new activity**. If the address
   was compromised or re-assigned and the activity has stopped, it will drop off without any action.
2. **Ask us to remove it now.** Open an issue using the *Removal request* template
   (<https://github.com/kotivahdit/blocklist/issues/new?template=removal.md>) or e-mail **kotivahdit@proton.me** with:
   * the IP address,
   * why you think the listing is wrong (e.g. the address was re-assigned to you, a misconfigured service has been fixed),
   * optionally, a contact address for a reply.

We aim to answer within **72 hours**. Removal is free and requires no account with us.

* If the listing was **our mistake**, we remove it, add the address to a 30-day protection list and record the case in
  [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md).
* If the activity is **ongoing** (for example a compromised device still scanning), we will tell you what we observed
  (time, port, type of activity) so you can find the cause – we will not publish details about you.

We cannot remove entries from third-party databases (AbuseIPDB, abuse.ch, GreyNoise …); please use their own processes.
