# Privacy

Our sensors record the **source IP address, timestamps and the data a connecting client sends** (e.g. usernames tried,
commands, HTTP requests). Under EU law an IP address can be personal data.

* **Purpose / legal basis:** network and information security – detecting and reducing abuse (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f),
  recital 49).
* **What we publish:** IP addresses of sources that attacked our sensors (blocklists), aggregated statistics, and
  indicators such as malware URLs. We never publish passwords that might be real, keys, full session contents or
  any data about uninvolved people.
* **Retention:** raw sensor logs ≤ 30 days (automatic daily cleanup; T-Pot database 30 days); blocklist entries expire 7 days after last activity; aggregated statistics
  (counts, countries) are kept.
* **Sharing:** with the security organisations listed in [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md#4-outbound-reporting).
* **Your rights:** to ask what we hold about an address or to request removal, see [REMOVAL.md](REMOVAL.md) or e-mail
  kotivahdit@proton.me.

Legitimate users have no reason to connect to our sensors; they do not host any real service or website for the public.
